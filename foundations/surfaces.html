Panels and surfaces

Everything sits on panels of dark glass over the moving gradient. Content panels carry Jewel’s signature corner: two hairlines cut through the top right.

CSS
css/components/panel.css, css/components/knockout.css, css/background.css

01 — Glass and solid

Two surfaces

Glass (the default) is 86% near-black with a 24px blur, so the colour behind it glows through. Solid is fully opaque. Same panel, same text colours; only the surface tokens change.

Glass · default

The background glows through, softly.

Solid · data-panel="solid"

Nothing shows through.

When to use solid

  • Over photography, where colour showing through would compete with the photo.
  • For print, where blur doesn’t exist.
  • Where blur is too costly: very large panels on low-powered phones.
  • Everywhere else, use glass. Put data-panel="solid" on any element and everything inside it switches.

02 — Panel shapes

Content panel

Any panel directly inside .panel-stack, or one with .panel--content: square top left, a 2px top right with the knockout lines, and 10px bottom corners.

Content panel.

Plain panel

Every other panel is square: cards, nested panels, the header.

Plain panel.

Knockout on anything

.knockout cuts the two lines into any element. .no-knockout removes them from a content panel. The lines are real holes, so the background shows through.

03 — Background

The gradient

The five Jewel colours drift slowly behind every page. It’s the only expressive element in the system. It freezes for people who prefer reduced motion, and data-bg="paused" on <html> stops it.

Markup

Put the background and the cap first in <body>. The cap hides content scrolling in the gap above the sticky header.

04 — Shadow

Almost none

Separation comes from glass and hairlines. One soft shadow exists for things that float over content.

--shadow-none

--shadow-float

05 — Usage

Use it for

  • Glass panels for all content.
  • Content panels in a .panel-stack, with the gradient showing between them.
  • Solid panels over photos and in print.

Avoid

  • Panels inside panels with their own backgrounds. Nest plain blocks, or use hairlines.
  • Shadows to separate things.
  • Pop-ups that hang outside a content panel. The knockout mask clips them; open them with Jewel.float().
