Motion

Quick and quiet. Things fade, slide a little and settle; nothing bounces. The background is the only thing that moves on its own.

Tokens
--duration-*, --ease-*

01 — Durations

Three speeds

Fast for hovers and presses, base for most changes, slow for things entering the page. Press Play to compare.

--duration-fast
--duration-base
--duration-slow

02 — Easing

Two curves

Standard for things that move within the page. Out for things arriving: they start fast and settle.

--ease-standard
--ease-out

03 — Reduced motion

Respect the setting

  • When someone asks for reduced motion, transitions drop to almost nothing, the background freezes, video never autoplays, and charts appear finished.
  • Components handle this themselves. In your own CSS, wrap any movement in @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference), or read Jewel.reducedMotion.matches in a script.

04 — Usage

Use it for

  • Fades and short slides that explain where something came from.
  • The duration tokens, and one of the two curves.

Avoid

  • Bounces, springs and parallax.
  • Motion longer than --duration-slow, except the background and loading indicators.
  • Animating layout (width, height) when opacity or a transform will do.
