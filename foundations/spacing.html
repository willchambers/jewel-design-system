Spacing and sizing

A 4px spacing scale, a handful of control heights and icon sizes, and two line weights. Use the tokens and every gap on a page agrees.

Stack and cluster

Spacing between elements comes from the parent, never from margins on the children. .stack spaces a column; .cluster spaces a row that wraps. Set the gap with --stack-gap or --cluster-gap.

03 — Icon sizes

Icons

Icons are hairline outlines (--icon-stroke, 1.25) in the text color. 16px inside buttons and fields, 22px in the header and the floating button, 28px in drop zones and empty states.

05 — Rules

Lines

A 1px hairline for edges and dividers. A 2px rule marks what’s selected or current: the open tab, the current page, a notice’s tone, a focused field.

--hairline
--rule-strong

06 — Usage

Use it for

  • The spacing tokens for every margin, padding and gap.
  • .stack and .cluster to space groups of elements.
  • --control-h* for anything a person presses, and --hit-min for its tap area.

Avoid

  • Pixel values, or numbers between the steps of the scale.
  • Margins on elements inside a .stack. The stack sets the gap.
  • Controls shorter than 44px on touch screens.
