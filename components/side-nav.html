Side nav

A vertical list of links in labeled groups, with groups that fold away. On wide screens it’s a column beside the content; on phones the same nav opens as a drawer from the header’s menu button.

02 — In a page

Column and drawer

Wrap the page in .with-side-nav for the column, which shows from 64rem. Put the same nav in a .sheet--start dialog for the drawer.

03 — Usage

Use it for

  • Sites and apps with more sections than fit in the header.
  • Two levels at most: headings, then a group.

Avoid

  • A handful of pages. The header is enough.
  • Actions such as Log out. Keep the nav to places.

04 — Accessibility

Notes

  • Give each nav a distinct aria-label (“Sections”, “Primary”).
  • Groups are native <details>, so they open with Enter or Space.
  • In the drawer, focus stays inside until it closes; following a link or widening the screen closes it.
