Checkbox, radio and switch

Choices for yes or no, one of several, or several of several. The whole row is the label, at least 44px tall, so it’s easy to hit.

CSS
css/components/choice.css

01 — Variants

Checkboxes

Any number of options, each on or off.

Radio buttons

One option from a few. .field__options--inline lays them out in a row.

Switch

A checkbox with role="switch", for a setting that takes effect straight away.

02 — Usage

Use it for

  • Checkboxes for independent options, and for agreeing to something.
  • Radios for two to five options where people should see them all.
  • Switches for settings that apply at once, without a Save button.

Avoid

  • A switch inside a form that only applies on submit. Use a checkbox.
  • Radios for long lists. Use a select.

03 — Accessibility

Notes

  • Native inputs inside their label: the whole row is clickable and announced correctly.
  • Group related choices with role="group" or role="radiogroup" and a label, or a <fieldset> and <legend>.
