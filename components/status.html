Status

A dot and a word for the state of one thing: a pod, a job, a deploy. The traffic light colors everyone already reads as status, with the word always saying the state, so color is never the only signal.

02 — Usage

Use it for

  • The state of a thing: running, pending, failed, unknown.
  • Status columns in tables and lists.
  • Small status next to a title, such as a cluster’s health.

Avoid

  • Status colors for decoration or for chart series. They always mean something; chart colors never do.
  • A dot with no word. Color alone fails people who can’t tell the hues apart.
  • Long messages. Use a .notice with the matching tone.

03 — Accessibility

Notes

  • The word carries the meaning; the dot only reinforces it.
  • Every status text tone is at least 7.19:1 on glass and every mark at least 3.05:1. The numbers are in docs/accessibility.md.
  • When a status changes while the page is open, put it inside role="status" or aria-live="polite" so screen readers hear it.
