02 — Usage

Use it for

  • Sites more than two levels deep.
  • The top of the page, above the title.

Avoid

  • Flat sites with one level.
  • Showing the steps of a process. That’s a stepper.

03 — Accessibility

Notes

  • A nav labelled “Breadcrumb” around an ordered list.
  • The current page is a span with aria-current="page", not a link.
