Video

A figure whose media is a muted, looping clip with a play and pause button. It plays while on screen and never autoplays for people who prefer reduced motion.

CSS
css/components/video.css
JS
js/components/video.js

01 — Example

Clip

Replace the artwork with your clip’s <source>. The poster frame shows until it plays.

02 — Usage

Use it for

  • Short silent loops: a motion reel, a product in use.

Avoid

  • Video with sound or speech. Use a player with controls and captions.
  • Several loops playing at once in view.

03 — Accessibility

Notes

  • The button’s label follows the state: “Play video” or “Pause video”. Once paused by hand, it stays paused.
  • It pauses when scrolled away and never autoplays with reduced motion.
