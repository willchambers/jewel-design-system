Accordion

Stacked sections that open in place. It’s built on native details elements, so it works without a script and find-in-page opens the right section.

CSS
css/components/accordion.css
JS
js/components/accordion.js (optional)

01 — Variants

One open at a time

Give every item the same name, and opening one closes the others. A label can sit at the right of a heading.

What sizes can I upload?

JPG, PNG, HEIC or WebP, up to 20 MB.

Who can see my photos? Privacy

Anyone with the link, unless you make the post private.

Can I sell prints?

Not yet.

Flush

.accordion--flush drops the outer rules, for inside a list or against a panel’s edge. Without a shared name, several can be open.

03 — Usage

Use it for

  • Questions and answers, settings groups, long details people only sometimes need.

Avoid

  • Content everyone needs. Don’t hide it.
  • A single item. Use a link or show it.

04 — Accessibility

Notes

  • Native <details> and <summary>: screen readers announce expanded and collapsed.
  • Keep headings short; the summary is read every time someone tabs past it.
