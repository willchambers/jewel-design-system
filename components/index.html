Components

Every component in Jewel, one page each, with live examples, copy-paste code, usage notes and accessibility notes. Each is one CSS file, and a script only where CSS can’t do the job.

01 — Actions

  • ButtonPrimary, ghost, text and icon buttons, plus a two-state toggle.
  • Floating action buttonThe fixed button for the page’s main action, with an optional label.
  • TagLabels, linked tags and pressable tags, in two sizes.
  • BadgeStatus and count labels, on their own or over photos.

02 — Navigation

  • HeaderThe sticky header, in four layouts, with a phone menu.
  • Side navGrouped page links: a column on wide screens, a drawer on phones.
  • BreadcrumbsThe trail from the home page to this one.
  • TabsSwitch between views of the same thing, in place.
  • FooterThe small print at the foot of every page.

03 — Disclosure and overlays

  • AccordionSections that open in place, such as questions and answers.
  • DropdownA button that opens a short menu of actions or choices.
  • SheetTearsheet, modal and drawer dialogs on a native dialog.
  • LightboxA full-screen photo viewer with arrows, swipes and captions.

04 — Inputs

  • Text fieldText inputs, text areas and selects, with labels, hints and errors.
  • Checkbox, radio and switchCheckboxes, radio buttons and switches.
  • Tag inputA field that turns typed words into tags.
  • Drop zonePick one photo by dragging it in or browsing, with a preview.
  • Date pickerType a date or pick one from a calendar.
  • SearchA search box with clear, a shortcut key and suggestions.
  • FilterTag buttons that show only matching items.

05 — Feedback

  • NoticeInfo, update and error messages in the page.
  • ProgressA thin bar that shows how far along a task is.
  • Empty stateWhat to show when there’s nothing here yet.

06 — Content

  • FigurePhotos and artwork with a hairline caption.
  • VideoA muted, looping clip with a play and pause control.
  • Index listNumbered rows of projects or posts.
  • Meta listLabel and value rows for the facts about something.
  • QuoteA pull quote with its source.
  • Code snippetCode blocks with a copy button, and inline code.

07 — Data and AI

  • StatKey numbers, stacked, inline or as one hero figure.
  • Data tableSortable, selectable tables of records.
  • ChartLine, area, bar, pie, donut and sparkline charts from a table.
  • ChatThe shell for an AI assistant: conversation, prompts and composer.
