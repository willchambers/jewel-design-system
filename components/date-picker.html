Date picker

A text field you can type a date into, with a calendar button for when you’d rather pick. Typing follows the page’s language; the calendar works from the keyboard.

02 — Behaviour

Script

NameDetail
TypingFollows the page’s language: lang="en" is month/day/year, lang="en-GB" day/month/year. ISO dates always work.
Calendar keysArrows move a day or a week, Home and End the week’s ends, Page Up and Page Down a month (with Shift, a year), Enter picks, Esc closes.
Optionsdata-min, data-max (a date or today), data-week-start, data-value.
ValueThe hidden input submits YYYY-MM-DD.
jewel:datechangeDetail { date, value }. API: el.jewelDatepicker.value, .open(), .close().

03 — Usage

Use it for

  • Dates people may not know by heart, or need in a range.

Avoid

  • Birthdays and other dates people know. Let them type.
  • Date ranges. It picks one date.

04 — Accessibility

Notes

  • The calendar is a grid people move through with the arrow keys; each day is read in full (“Monday 5 October 2026”).
  • An unreadable or out-of-range date shows as a field error.
