Date picker

A text field you can type a date into, with a calendar button for when you’d rather pick. Typing follows the page’s language; the calendar works from the keyboard.

03 — Usage

Use it for

  • Dates people may not know by heart, or need in a range.

Avoid

  • Birthdays and other dates people know. Let them type.
  • Date ranges. It picks one date.

04 — Accessibility

Notes

  • The calendar is a grid people move through with the arrow keys; each day is read in full (“Monday 5 October 2026”).
  • An unreadable or out-of-range date shows as a field error.

05 — Without Jewel’s JS

What the script does

The script reads typed dates, builds the calendar, moves through it from the keyboard and writes the ISO date into the hidden input. The CSS reads only the markup below, so a framework can render the calendar from its own state and leave out datepicker.js and data-component.

Events and keyboard

  • The toggle, or Alt+↓ in the field, opens the calendar with focus on the chosen day, or today.
  • In the grid, ← → move a day, ↑ ↓ a week, Home and End to the week’s ends, Page Up and Page Down a month (with Shift, a year). Move focus and tabindex="0" together.
  • Picking a day, Today, or Esc closes it and returns focus to the toggle. A press outside, or focus leaving, closes it without moving focus. Clear closes it and focuses the field.
  • The script reads typed dates in the page language’s order (lang="en" month/day/year, lang="en-GB" day/month/year) or ISO, and tidies them on blur. jewel:datechange doesn’t fire without it.
