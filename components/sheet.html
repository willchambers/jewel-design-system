Sheet

A sheet is a dialog. It slides up from the bottom as a tearsheet, sits in the middle as a modal, or slides in from the left as a drawer. The insides are the same in all three.

01 — Variants

Tearsheet, modal and drawers

Each button opens a real sheet. The left drawer here is the site’s menu; the right one shows the details of one record.

Details drawer

.sheet--end slides in from the right, for the details of one thing: a pod, an order, a file. Its title is a heading, with a small label above it (.sheet__eyebrow) for the kind of thing or where it lives. A short form title such as “New post” can stay a label.

Markup

Swap .sheet--bottom for .sheet--center, .sheet--start or .sheet--end. Any element with data-sheet-open="id" opens it; data-sheet-close closes it.

03 — Usage

Use it for

  • Tearsheet: a task people finish and leave, such as a post form. Best on phones.
  • Modal: a short decision or a small form.
  • Drawer: navigation on phones.

Avoid

  • Content people need to read alongside the page.
  • A sheet that opens another sheet.
  • Warnings that block for no reason. Use a notice in the page.

04 — Accessibility

Notes

  • A native <dialog>, labeled by its title with aria-labelledby.
  • The close button is an icon button with aria-label="Close".

05 — Without Jewel’s JS

What the script does

The script opens and closes the <dialog>, asks before discarding a changed form, stops the page scrolling and returns focus. The CSS reads only the markup below, so a framework that owns open and closed can drive it and leave out sheet.js and data-component.

StateSet thisNotes
Opendialog.showModal()Not the open attribute alone: the backdrop, top layer and inert page come from the modal. In React, call it and close() from an effect when your state changes.
Closeddialog.close()CSS slides it out once [open] goes.
Page scrollhas-open-sheet on <html> while it’s openStops the page scrolling behind it.
Height on phones--sheet-vh on the dialog, in pxOptional. The script sets it to visualViewport.height so the sheet shrinks above the on-screen keyboard. Without it, 100dvh.
Discard bar<div class="sheet__confirm" role="alertdialog"> after .sheet__headOptional. The script adds one with Keep editing and Discard buttons, hidden until a changed form is closed.
OpenersYour own click handlersdata-sheet-open and data-sheet-close are only read by the script.

Events and keyboard

  • Esc fires the dialog’s cancel event. Call preventDefault() if you want to ask first; either way, update your state on the dialog’s close event.
  • A click on the backdrop lands on the dialog itself (event.target === dialog). The script also checks that the press started there, so a text selection dragged outside doesn’t close it.
  • On open, the script focuses [autofocus] or the first field in .sheet__body, except on touch screens. On close, return focus to the button that opened it.
  • While it’s open, keep the focused field in view once the on-screen keyboard is up.
  • jewel:sheetopen and jewel:sheetclose don’t fire without the script.
