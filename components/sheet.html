Sheet

A sheet is a dialog. It slides up from the bottom as a tearsheet, sits in the middle as a modal, or slides in from the left as a drawer. The insides are the same in all three.

CSS
css/components/sheet.css
JS
js/components/sheet.js

01 — Variants

Tearsheet, modal and drawer

Each button opens a real sheet. The drawer here is the site’s menu.

Markup

Swap .sheet--bottom for .sheet--center or .sheet--start. Any element with data-sheet-open="id" opens it; data-sheet-close closes it.

02 — Behavior

Script

NameDetail
FocusMoves into the sheet, stays there, and returns to the button that opened it.
ClosingEsc, the close button, or a click on the dimmed page. A form with unsaved changes asks first.
PhonesThe tearsheet goes full height and shrinks above the on-screen keyboard. The page can’t scroll behind it.
jewel:sheetopen, jewel:sheetcloseFire on the dialog.
dialog.jewelSheet.open(opener), .close({ force }), .requestClose().

03 — Usage

Use it for

  • Tearsheet: a task people finish and leave, such as a post form. Best on phones.
  • Modal: a short decision or a small form.
  • Drawer: navigation on phones.

Avoid

  • Content people need to read alongside the page.
  • A sheet that opens another sheet.
  • Warnings that block for no reason. Use a notice in the page.

04 — Accessibility

Notes

  • A native <dialog>, labeled by its title with aria-labelledby.
  • The close button is an icon button with aria-label="Close".
