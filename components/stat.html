Stat

The key numbers in a report or dashboard: a small uppercase label and a large semibold value, in the same Inter, with no color on the number.

02 — Usage

Use it for

  • Three to eight numbers that matter most.
  • Values written as they should be read: “40+”, “3.2M”.
  • .stat__unit for %, K, M and units; .stat__note for one line of context.

Avoid

  • Color on the number to mean good or bad. Say it in the note.
  • More than one hero per page.
