Background picker

Lets the viewer choose the lava lamp’s palette and motion, and remembers the choice. A settings control for apps that stay open all day.

01 — Variants

Palette and motion

Each option is a pressable tag that carries the attribute it sets on <html>. Try it: this page changes. data-bg-store remembers the choice on this device.

Palettes only

Offer just the options that suit the product. The groups are optional, and so is the label when there’s one group.

Your own palette

Write a palette as a data-palette rule and add a button for it. The swatch reads the same tokens, so it needs no color of its own.

[data-palette="harbor"] { --bg-1: …; --bg-2: …; --bg-3: …; --bg-4: …; --bg-5: …; --bg-base: …; }

02 — What it switches

Wax blobs

The Motion group needs the wax layer in the page: one after .jewel-bg and a copy inside .jewel-cap, so the strip above the header matches. It shows only with data-bg-motion="wax" on <html>.

<div class="jewel-wax" aria-hidden="true"><i></i><i></i><i></i><i></i><i></i></div>

Your own image or video

A product can also offer its own backgrounds: a photo, a render or a looping clip in .jewel-media, right after .jewel-bg. The picker doesn’t insert media for you, because where the files come from is the product’s business. Add your own buttons beside it, insert or remove the layer, and set a palette close to the media’s colors so the gradient that shows while it loads matches.

<div class="jewel-media" aria-hidden="true"><img class="jewel-media__still" src="night-sky.webp" alt=""></div>

03 — Usage

Use it for

  • Dashboards and tools that stay open for hours, where a calmer or cooler backdrop is a comfort.
  • A settings sheet or page, reached from a menu.
  • A short list: the palettes that suit the product, not every one.

Avoid

  • The header or a floating button. The background is a setting, not navigation.
  • Using a palette to mean something. Status has its own colors.
  • A light option. Jewel is dark-only; every palette sits under the same glass.

04 — Accessibility

Notes

  • Options are buttons with aria-pressed; each group has role="group" and a name.
  • The swatch is decoration (aria-hidden); the option’s name is its text.
  • Text contrast doesn’t change with the palette: glass is tested over near-white and near-black, and every palette color sits between them.
  • With reduced motion the lava lamp, the wax blobs and the drift of a still all stop. The palette still changes.

05 — Without Jewel’s JS

In React and other frameworks

  • Keep the palette and motion in your own state. Set data-palette and data-bg-motion on <html> from it, and remove each for its default (jewel, lava).
  • Render the same markup and set each button’s aria-pressed.
  • To avoid a flash of the default on load, restore a saved choice from a small inline script in <head>.
