Empty state

What to show when there’s nothing here yet: a fresh account, an empty album, a search with no results. A quiet icon, a short title, one line of help and one way forward.

CSS
css/components/empty-state.css

01 — Variants

First use

A new page with nothing in it. Offer the one action that fills it.

No photos yet

Post your first photo and it shows up here.

Boxed

.empty-state--boxed frames an empty region inside a page. Add .knockout for the corner.

This album is empty

Add photos from your library, or import a folder.

No results

Say what was searched, and offer to clear it.

No photos match “harbour at noon”

Check the spelling or try fewer words.

02 — Usage

Use it for

  • Every list, table and gallery that can be empty.
  • One or two actions at most.

Avoid

  • A bare “Nothing here”.
  • Illustrations. Keep to one hairline icon.

03 — Accessibility

Notes

  • The title is a real heading at the right level for its place in the page.
  • The icon is decorative (aria-hidden="true").
