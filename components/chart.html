Chart

Line, area, bar, pie, donut and sparkline charts, drawn from a plain HTML table with no chart library. The table stays as the chart’s data view, so every number is readable without the chart.

01 — Chart types

Line chart

Change over time, for up to five series. Hover or tap a point for its card; focus the chart and use the arrow keys.

Stacked area chart

data-type="area" data-stacked: how parts add up to a total over time.

Bar chart

Comparing amounts across categories.

Grouped bar chart

Two or more series side by side in each category.

Pie chart

Parts of one whole, up to six slices.

Donut chart

A pie with the total in the middle. Set the centre with data-center-label.

Sparkline

A tiny trend line for inside a stat. It takes its data from attributes and says the trend in its aria-label.

data-heightPlot height in px (default 260).
data-center-label, data-center-valueThe donut’s centre (default: Total and the sum).

03 — Usage

Use it for

  • The first column for categories; each further column is a series.
  • Cells written as they should read (“42%”, “120,400”). The hover card shows your text.
  • A title on every chart that says what it shows.

Avoid

  • More than five series. The sixth turns grey; fold extras into Other or split the chart.
  • Series colors on text.
  • Pie charts with more than six slices.

04 — Accessibility

Notes

  • Every chart keeps its table behind a “Show data” link, and the table is all that shows without a script.
  • Charts can be tabbed to; arrows step through points and a live region reads each one.
  • The palette is checked for color-blind contrast, and lines are labelled at their ends where they don’t collide.
