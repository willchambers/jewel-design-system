Tabs

Tabs switch between views of the same thing, in place, without leaving the page. Tabs sit in one box, and the open tab is filled with a 2px accent rule along its bottom.

03 — Usage

Use it for

  • Two to six views of the same thing: details, comments, a map.
  • Short labels, one or two words.

Avoid

  • Steps in a process. People expect to go back and forth freely.
  • Navigation between pages. Use links.
  • Content people need to compare side by side.

04 — Accessibility

Notes

  • Uses the ARIA tabs pattern: tablist, tab and tabpanel, with aria-selected.
  • Only the open tab is in the tab order; arrows move between tabs.
