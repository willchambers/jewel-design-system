Tag input

A field that turns what people type into tags. Enter or a comma adds one; Backspace removes the last. It can suggest tags and stop at a limit.

    03 — Usage

    Use it for

    • Free-form tags people make up as they go.

    Avoid

    • Picking from a fixed list. Use checkboxes or a filter.
