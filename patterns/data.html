02 — Built from

  • StatThe hero figure and the row of key numbers.
  • ChartThe trend, and the sparkline in a stat.
  • Data tableThe detail, sortable.

03 — Guidelines

Use it for

  • Lead with the one number people came for.
  • Say what each number covers: the period, the place.
  • A chart for the shape of the data, a table for the exact values.

Avoid

  • Colour on numbers to mean good or bad. Use words: “up a third”.
  • A chart for one or two numbers. Use stats.
  • Dashboards where every panel shouts. One hero per page.
