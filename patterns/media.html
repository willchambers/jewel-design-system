02 — Built from

03 — Guidelines

Use it for

  • One wide lead image, then a rhythm of smaller ones.
  • Captions that add context; alt text that describes.
  • Silent loops only, paused for reduced motion.

Avoid

  • Several clips playing at once.
  • Text over photos, except badges.
