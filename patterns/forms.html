Forms

A form asks for a few things, in a sensible order, and tells people straight away what’s wrong and how to fix it. Jewel forms are one column of hairline fields, grouped under small labels.

02 — Anatomy

The parts

PartWhat it does
form.form[data-component="form"][novalidate]The form. novalidate hands validation to form.js, which shows Jewel’s own messages.
.form__group and .form__legendA fieldset with a hairline above and a small label, for each group of related fields.
.form__row--2Two short fields side by side from 48rem; one column on phones.
.form__error, .form__statusThe form’s own messages, styled as notices.
.form__actionsButtons, right-aligned, with the main action last.
jewel:submitFires on a valid submit with { form, formData, waitUntil(promise) }. The button shows data-busy-label until the promise settles, then the form shows data-success or the error.

04 — Guidelines

Use it for

  • One column, in the order people think of the answers.
  • Labels above fields; mark either the required or the optional ones, whichever is fewer.
  • Validate on submit, then as people fix each field.
  • A button label that says what happens: Send, Post, Save changes.

Avoid

  • Asking for anything you don’t need.
  • Disabling the submit button until the form is valid. People can’t tell why.
  • Clearing what people typed after an error.
