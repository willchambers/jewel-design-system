Posting a photo

The whole flow for adding a photo: a floating button opens a sheet, the post form takes the photo and its details, and the page gets one event with everything in it.

JS
form.js, tag-input.js, dropzone.js, post-form.js, sheet.js

01 — Try it

The flow

Press the + button in the bottom-right corner, or open the same form as a tearsheet or a modal. Close it with unsaved changes to see the guard, or make the upload fail to see the error.

Event log · jewel:post

Nothing posted yet.

Markup

The floating button, then the sheet holding the post form. Listen for jewel:post and call waitUntil() with your upload.

02 — The event

jewel:post

DetailWhat it holds
fileThe photo, as a File.
title, description, location, altStrings.
tagsAn array of strings.
publishTrue or false, from the switch.
formData, formFor sending as is.
waitUntil(promise)Keeps the form busy. Resolve to show “Posted.” and reset; reject to show the error and keep the draft.

03 — Built from

  • Floating action buttonOpens the sheet from anywhere on the page.
  • SheetTearsheet on phones, modal on wide screens; guards unsaved drafts.
  • Drop zoneThe photo, with a preview.
  • Text fieldTitle, description, location and alt text.
  • Tag inputTags, with suggestions.
  • Checkbox, radio and switchPublish to site.

04 — Guidelines

Use it for

  • Ask for the photo first; everything else follows from it.
  • Make alt text easy: suggest a draft, never block posting on it.
  • Keep the draft when an upload fails, and say how to retry.

Avoid

  • Required fields beyond the photo and a title.
  • Closing the sheet on a failed upload.
